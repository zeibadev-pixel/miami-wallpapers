This privacy policy applies to the Android apps **Vice Sunset Icons** (com.zeiba.vicesunset) and **Vice Sunset Icons Lite** (com.zeiba.vicesunset.lite), published as "Zeiba Developer".

## 2. Summary

The apps are icon packs. They contain **no ads, no analytics or tracking services** and require **no user account**. We do not collect, store or sell personal data on our own servers.

## 3. Processing on your device

To show which apps already have an icon, the app reads the list of apps installed on your device (app names and activity names). This happens **only locally on your device**. The list is not transmitted automatically.

## 4. Icon requests (full version only)

If you voluntarily send an icon request, the app creates an email to zeibadev@gmail.com and opens your email app. The email contains:

- the names and activity names of the apps you selected,
- the app version, device model and Android version,
- your email address (as the sender).

The email is only sent when you send it yourself in your email app. We use this data solely to create the requested icons and, if needed, to reply to you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Emails are deleted when no longer needed, at the latest after 12 months. We use Gmail (Google Ireland Limited) to receive emails.

## 5. Wallpapers

Wallpapers are loaded from GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA; raw.githubusercontent.com) when you open the wallpaper section. For technical reasons, your IP address is transmitted to GitHub. We do not receive this data. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## 6. Google Play

The apps are distributed via Google Play. Google Play services are used to verify the license (full version) and for the optional in-app review feature. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 7. Permissions

- **Internet / network state:** to load wallpapers.
- **Set wallpaper:** to apply a wallpaper at your request.
- **Storage (older Android versions):** to save a wallpaper you download.
- **Notifications:** for app notices, e.g. after a download.
- **License check / billing (Google Play):** to verify your purchase.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Contact zeibadev@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

## 9. Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect data from them.

This privacy policy may be updated if the apps or legal requirements change. The version published here applies.
